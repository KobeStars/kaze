## At a glance

- **Keyboard first**: a command palette (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>) to do everything without the mouse.
- **Made for coding**: a built-in code editor, a multi-device responsive mode and the developer tools.
- **Privacy first**: ad and tracker blocker, built-in VPN over Tor, your data stays on your PC.
- **Make it yours**: 14 style templates, 6 themes, 20 home backgrounds, your own fonts and CSS.
- **Compatible**: Chrome extensions, Netflix and Spotify, automatic updates.

## Features

Every feature is named after a wind.

| | Feature | What it does |
|:-:|---|---|
| 息 | **Souffle** (breath) | The command palette: open a site, find a tab, search your history, switch theme… <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> |
| 双 | **Split view** | Two sites side by side, with an adjustable divider. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd> |
| 端 | **Responsive mode** | Your site on a phone, a tablet and a laptop at the same time; scrolling, clicks and typing stay in sync. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd> |
| 流 | **Courants** (currents) | Workspaces with their own tabs, name, color and icon. |
| 盾 | **Bouclier** (wind shield) | Ad and tracker blocker (EasyList and EasyPrivacy lists), can be turned off per site. |
| 霧 | **Brume** (mist) | The built-in VPN, free and with no account: your tabs go through the Tor network, exit country of your choice. |
| 微 | **Brise** (breeze) | Puts inactive tabs to sleep to free memory. |
| 嵐 | **Tempête** (storm) | Focus mode: distracting sites are blocked during your session. |
| 側 | **Side panel** | Your pinned web apps on the right (Discord, Notion, Claude…). |
| 拡 | **Chrome extensions** | Paste a Chrome Web Store link: Kaze installs the extension, with its button and popup. |
| 映 | **Netflix and Spotify** | Protected content (Widevine) plays like in Chrome. |
| 縦 | **Vertical tabs** | Tabs on the left, right, top or bottom; drag and drop, middle-click to close. |

## Kaze Studio 筆

A code editor inside the browser (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>E</kbd>): open a local folder or file and see the site next to the code, updated as you type.

- The VS Code engine (Monaco): highlighting, autocompletion, Emmet, multiple cursors
- Live preview, hot-reloaded CSS, built-in console
- Small local server: relative paths, `fetch` and ES modules work
- “Open in Kaze Studio” copies a web page so you can experiment with it locally
- Formatting, snippets and project export as .zip

## Install

1. Download **`Kaze-Setup-x.y.z.exe`** from the [latest release](https://github.com/KobeStars/kaze/releases/latest).
2. Run it and choose the install folder.
3. That's it: Kaze then **updates itself** in the background.

> [!NOTE]
> **A blue “Windows protected your PC” screen?** The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't know it. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Only once.

**Requirements**: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Every shortcut can be changed in the settings.

## Privacy 守

- **Nothing in the cloud**: bookmarks, history, settings and tabs stay on your PC. No account, no telemetry.
- **Isolated pages**: every site runs in a sandbox, with no access to Windows or your files.
- **Permissions on request**: camera, microphone, location, notifications; Kaze asks you for each site.
- **Brume** routes your tabs through Tor, and nothing leaves with your real address if Tor drops.

Kaze only connects on its own to GitHub (updates), Ghostery (blocker lists, at most weekly) and Google (the Widevine module). Details: [privacy policy](https://kobestars.github.io/kaze/politique.html#confidentialite) (in French).
