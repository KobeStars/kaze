Kaze — license and privacy

PRIVACY
Kaze has no account, no ads, no telemetry and no usage statistics. Your bookmarks, history, cookies, settings and tabs stay on your PC (%APPDATA%\Kaze). Kordix Studio receives no data.

Kaze connects on its own to:
- GitHub, to check for and download its updates (can be turned off in Settings > About > Automatic updates);
- Ghostery, to download the Shield's filter lists, at most once a week;
- Google, to download the Widevine module (Netflix, Spotify).
Everything else (sites, search, Brume/Tor, weather, Bing image of the day, fonts, extensions) only happens when you ask for it.

The installer adds Kaze to the "Open with" menu for .html and .htm files. It does not change your default browser.

Full policy (in French): https://kobestars.github.io/kaze/politique.html
